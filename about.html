What this is

GridBoard shows practice trades on Kraken. The money is not real, and nothing on this site can send an order.

There are 75 tokens priced in US dollars. Each one has two practice accounts, Neutral and Long. That makes 150 accounts, and each starts with $10,000.

Every buy and every sell pays a 0.80% fee. Buy and sell prices sit 5% apart, and each step is 5% from the price before it.

Neutral and Long

Both styles start with about half of the $10,000 already in the token.

Neutral then places three buy orders below the price and three sell orders above it. There are orders waiting whichever way the price moves.

Long places four buys below and two sells above, so more of its orders wait for a lower price. Its sells are small pieces of what it already holds.

When a trade happens

The orders wait, and any of them can trade the moment the market price reaches it. Nothing on a clock forces a trade.

After a buy, a sell goes up one step. After a sell, a buy goes down one step. The new order stays on the same 5% ladder.

Only an order that trades creates the next one. The tokens bought at the start do not.

Hot watch

A token that just traded goes on hot watch. Its two accounts are checked every few seconds instead of every half minute, for about 90 minutes after the last trade, and the board marks the name while that lasts.

The checker places the next order. This website only reads what it did.

When prices jump around

Every step is 5% today, and 5% is the smallest gap.

The plan is to widen the gap for a token that has been jumping around, so it trades less often and pays the fee less often. That is not running yet.

What the board shows

  • At work is the dollars in the token, Cash is what is waiting to buy, and Now is the two added together. The change from $10,000 includes the fees already paid.
  • Buy & hold is the same $10,000 bought once at that token’s Neutral start price and kept, after one 0.80% fee. All of it is at work, so there is no cash.
  • Buy · sell is how many times that token’s two accounts have bought and sold.
  • Hot watch lists the tokens that just traded.

The page only reads a saved update. There is no login, and the browser never connects to Kraken.
